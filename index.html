KNOW THE
FUNDAMENTALS.

01 — Move with purpose

W A S D to move. Mouse to aim. Hold Shift to sprint. Use the walls and crates as cover. Arrow keys also turn your view.

02 — Make your shots count

Click to fire. R to reload. Keep the crosshair on an enemy and manage your ammunition. Your weapon affects damage, fire rate, and magazine size.

03 — Clear the compound

Use West Approach, Mid Connector, or East Market to flank through Sun Court and Blue Court. A and B are navigation landmarks, not bomb sites. Eliminate all three enemy bots to win a round. First team to three wins the match. You have 90 seconds per round. Watch the radar for nearby enemies.

04 — Take a breather

Escape pauses the game and releases your mouse. Use free practice to explore without enemies. Adjust resolution in settings for slower hardware.
